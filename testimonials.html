Testimonial Guidelines

What makes a great testimonial?

  • Be specific - Mention particular aspects you appreciated
  • Share results - How did the project impact your business?
  • Be authentic - Write in your own voice and style
  • Focus on collaboration - What was it like working together?

What to include:

  • Your name and company/role
  • Project type and name
  • Your experience working together
  • Specific results or achievements
  • What stood out about the service
