About Cyber Runner 2077

Cyber Runner 2077 is a first-person parkour adventure set in the sprawling cyberpunk metropolis of Neo-Tokyo. Navigate towering skyscrapers, evade corporate security, and uncover the secrets of the city's AI overlord.

With fluid movement mechanics, stunning visuals, and an immersive storyline, Cyber Runner 2077 delivers an unforgettable experience that pushes the boundaries of browser-based gaming.

Game Features

Fluid Parkour System

Advanced movement mechanics with wall running, sliding, vaulting, and precision jumping.

Open World Exploration

Massive cyberpunk city with distinct districts, hidden areas, and vertical exploration.

AI-Driven Enemies

Smart enemy AI with adaptive behaviors, patrol routes, and tactical responses.

Environmental Puzzles

Challenging puzzles that require creative use of parkour skills and cybernetic abilities.

Cyber Enhancements

Upgrade your character with cybernetic implants that grant special abilities and skills.

Branching Narrative

Multiple story paths and endings based on your choices and gameplay style.
